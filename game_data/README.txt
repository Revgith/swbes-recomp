Put your own copy of Star Wars Battlefront: Elite Squadron (PSP, EU, ULES-01214) here:
the .iso, or its extracted files (a folder containing PSP_GAME).

On first start the .iso is unpacked into game_data\disc and the game's EBOOT is
decrypted into game_data\eboot.elf. A PPSSPP EBOOT dump placed here also works.

Saves, settings.ini and mods are kept in this folder too.
